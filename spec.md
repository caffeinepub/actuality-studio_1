# Specification

## Summary
**Goal:** Update Actuality Studio's site content to reflect Steiner's Seven Arts philosophy, replace the term "Seven Arts Unfoldment" with "The Sevenfold Arts," and add a disciplines card grid to the landing page.

**Planned changes:**
- Update the landing page hero and/or About section with evocative prose framing Actuality Studio around Steiner's philosophy of architecture as the supreme synthesis unfolding into the seven artistic disciplines
- Replace every instance of "Seven Arts Unfoldment" in all user-facing text with "The Sevenfold Arts"
- Add a card/grid section on the landing page titled "The Sevenfold Arts," displaying all seven disciplines (Music & Sound Design, Literature & Poetry, Movement Arts including contemporary eurythmy and performance, Sculpture & Ceramics, Painting & Digital Rendering, Drawing & Parametric Visualization, and allied crafts), each with a short evocative description and styled using the existing terracotta/sage/warm-gold palette and Cormorant Garamond typography

**User-visible outcome:** Visitors to the landing page see a clearly articulated Steinerian philosophy statement and a visual grid showcasing all seven artistic disciplines under the consistent label "The Sevenfold Arts" throughout the site.
